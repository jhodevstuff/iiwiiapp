Getting Started

Taking Photos

  1. Open IIWII
  2. Point at your subject
  3. Tap the shutter button (or press volume button)

Changing Filters

  • Quick: Swipe up from bottom
  • Full view: Long-press on preview
  • Tap any preset to apply

Features

Film Presets

IIWII includes carefully crafted presets inspired by classic film stocks. Each preset is applied in real-time — what you see is what you get.

Exposure Control

Use the edge slider on the right side of the screen. Slide up to brighten, down to darken.

Camera Switching

Double-tap anywhere on the camera preview to flip between front and back cameras.

Settings

Access settings by swiping up and tapping the gear icon.

Privacy

  • Save GPS to Photos: Add location to photo metadata
  • Location Metadata When Sharing: Keep location when sharing (off by default)

Interface

  • Transparent Background: Blurred camera behind UI
  • Gallery Button: Show/hide gallery shortcut
  • Edge EV Slider: Enable/disable exposure slider

Troubleshooting

Camera shows black screen

Check camera permission in Settings > IIWII > Camera. If enabled, try restarting the app or your iPhone.

Photos not saving

Ensure Photo Library permission is granted in Settings > IIWII > Photos. Also check available storage space.

Filter not applying

Make sure you're not using the "Default" preset (no filter). Check that intensity is not set to 0%.

FAQ

Where are my photos saved?

All photos are saved to your iPhone's Photo Library.

Does IIWII upload my photos anywhere?

No. All photos stay on your device. We have no servers or cloud storage.

Can I use IIWII offline?

Yes! IIWII works completely offline.

Does IIWII track me?

No. We collect zero data. No analytics, no tracking, no ads.

Why does the app need location access?

Only for optional GPS geotagging in photos. You can deny or disable it anytime.

Permissions

  • Camera (Required): To capture photos
  • Photo Library (Required): To save your photos
  • Location (Optional): For GPS geotagging
