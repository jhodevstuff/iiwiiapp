Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 2025

The short version: We don't collect, store, or share any of your personal data. Everything stays on your device.

Overview

IIWII ("the App") is a camera application developed by Joshua Hoffmann. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data the App accesses, how it's used, and your rights.

Data Collection

What We DO NOT Collect

  • No personal information
  • No usage analytics
  • No crash reports sent to us
  • No advertising data
  • No third-party tracking
  • No account creation required
  • No cloud uploads

What the App Accesses (On-Device Only)

Camera Access

  • Purpose: Required to capture photos
  • Storage: Photos are saved to your device's Photo Library
  • Sharing: Never transmitted or uploaded anywhere

Photo Library Access

  • Purpose: Save and display your captured photos
  • Storage: All photos remain in your local Photo Library
  • Sharing: Only when you explicitly share via the iOS share sheet

Location Access (Optional)

  • Purpose: Add GPS coordinates to photo metadata (geotagging)
  • Control: You can deny this permission or disable it in app settings
  • Storage: GPS data is embedded in photo EXIF metadata on your device only
  • Sharing: Location data is removed by default when sharing (configurable)

Data Storage

All data remains on your device:

  • Photos are stored in your iOS Photo Library
  • App preferences are stored locally on your device
  • No data is transmitted to external servers

Third-Party Services

IIWII uses no third-party services:

  • No analytics (Google Analytics, Firebase, etc.)
  • No advertising SDKs
  • No social media integrations
  • No crash reporting services

Your Control

You have full control over your data:

  • Camera Permission: Revoke in iOS Settings > IIWII > Camera
  • Photo Library Permission: Revoke in iOS Settings > IIWII > Photos
  • Location Permission: Revoke in iOS Settings > IIWII > Location
  • GPS in Photos: Disable in IIWII Settings > Privacy
  • Location When Sharing: Configure in IIWII Settings > Privacy

Data Sharing

We do not share any data with anyone because we don't collect any data.

When you use the iOS share sheet to share a photo, location metadata is removed by default. You can change this in app settings.

Children's Privacy

IIWII does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13. The app contains no inappropriate content and is rated 4+.
